Hark

Hear your important emails instead of watching for them.

Hark is a browser extension that reads new Gmail messages out loud, only from the senders you choose, the moment they arrive.

How it works

  1. Click Connect Gmail and pick any Google account.
  2. Type a sender, such as a company domain or an email address, and press Add. Add as many as you like.
  3. Hark checks for new unread mail about once a minute and speaks it aloud.

Features

  • Multiple senders at once
  • Read just the subject and intro, or the whole email
  • Adjustable speaking speed
  • Only new mail is read, never what is already in your inbox

Private by design

Hark has read-only access to Gmail and has no server. Your email is handled on your device. Read the privacy policy.
