Privacy policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026

Hark is a browser extension that reads new Gmail messages aloud. This policy explains what it accesses and what it does with that information.

What Hark accesses

When you connect a Google account, Hark asks for read-only access to your Gmail (the gmail.readonly scope). It uses this access to:

  • find unread messages from the senders you added, and
  • read each new message's sender name, subject and body so it can be spoken.

Hark cannot send, delete, move or change any email.

What Hark stores

The following is saved only on your device, in your browser's extension storage:

  • your settings (senders, speed, how much to read, on/off),
  • the email address of the connected account,
  • a temporary Google access token, and
  • the IDs of messages already read aloud, so none are repeated.

Email content is processed in memory to be spoken and is not saved.

What Hark shares

Hark has no servers and no analytics. The developer does not receive your email, your account details or any usage data. Hark does not sell, share or transfer your data to anyone, and does not use it for advertising.

Hark's only network requests go directly from your browser to Google, to sign you in and to read your Gmail.

Text-to-speech

Hark passes the text to be spoken to your browser's text-to-speech engine, which normally uses voices installed on your device. If you choose a network-based voice in your browser or system settings, that voice provider may process the text under its own policy.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Hark's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Your choices

  • Click Disconnect in Hark to sign out, revoke its access and clear its stored data.
  • You can also remove Hark's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Removing the extension deletes everything it stored.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new date.
